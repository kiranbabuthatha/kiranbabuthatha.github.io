2. Hosting and delivery

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) and delivered through Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., USA), which acts as a content delivery network and proxy. Cloudflare also decodes protected email links on the site.

When you open a page, both providers process your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested URL, the referring page and your browser’s user agent in server logs. This is technically necessary to deliver the site and to protect it against abuse and attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is a secure and reliably available website.

The two blog tutorials also load a code-highlighting script (highlight.js) from Cloudflare’s cdnjs service, which likewise transmits your IP address to Cloudflare.

Data may be transferred to the USA. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See the privacy statements of GitHub and Cloudflare.

3. Cookies and consent

On your first visit, a banner asks whether you agree to analytics. Nothing from Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager loads before you click “Accept”. If you click “Decline”, no analytics run.

Your choice is stored in your browser’s localStorage under the key kbt_analytics_consent, with the value granted or denied. This is strictly necessary to remember your choice so the banner does not reappear on every page (§ 25(2) TDDDG). If you use the light/dark switch, that preference is stored the same way under the key theme. Neither value is sent to any server.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page. Withdrawing consent deletes the _ga cookies and stops tracking.

4. Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4

If you consent, this site uses Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (parent company: Google LLC, USA). Both load only after you click “Accept”. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.

Purpose. To understand which pages are visited and which contact and booking links are used, in order to improve the site. Recorded events include page views and clicks on “Book a call”, email, phone and LinkedIn links.

Cookies. Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_Z27XQP488D, with a lifetime of up to 2 years.

Scope. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Google Signals and advertising features are not used. Data is retained in Google Analytics for 14 months.

BigQuery export. Event data is exported daily to Google BigQuery (Google Cloud) for my own analysis.

Transfer to the USA. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google Fonts

This site currently loads its fonts from Google’s servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When a page loads, your browser connects to Google, which transmits your IP address to Google. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is a consistent display of the site across devices.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Appointment booking

The Book a call page embeds Google Calendar appointment scheduling, provided by Google. Loading that page connects your browser to Google.

If you book a call, the data you enter (name, email address, chosen time and any notes) is processed by Google and by me to arrange the call. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: steps taken at your request before a possible contract.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

7. Contact by email, phone or LinkedIn

If you contact me, I use the data you send (such as your name, email address, phone number and message) only to reply and to prepare a possible engagement. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise; my legitimate interest is answering your enquiry.

I delete this data when it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods require me to keep it.

The LinkedIn link opens LinkedIn, which processes data under its own privacy policy.

8. No other use

I do not sell your data, do not use advertising tracking, and do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data (Art. 15),
  • have it corrected (Art. 16),
  • have it erased (Art. 17),
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18),
  • receive it in a portable format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21),
  • withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise any of these rights, email kiranbabu.thatha@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

10. Changes

I may update this policy when the site or the services it uses change. The current version always applies.

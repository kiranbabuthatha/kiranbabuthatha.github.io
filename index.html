02 About
A note from Kiran

I treat organic search as a product surface: measurable, testable, and accountable to real revenue.

My background sits at the seam between SEO strategy and applied data engineering. I started as a support engineer in banking and HR systems, resolving thousands of technical issues and automating over 200 manual processes, before moving to Germany for a Master's in Digital Engineering and joining Wayfair, where I scaled from intern to Senior Product Analyst.

At Wayfair, I worked on organic growth across five brands and eight international stores, partnering with Engineering, Product, and Content to align organic visibility with revenue. I shipped ML sentence-transformer models for internal linking, led page curation that cut indexable volume by 90% while keeping 98% of traffic, and built automation on the Claude and OpenAI APIs that lifted organic traffic by 5%. Since February 2026 I do this work independently for clients, alongside product experimentation and analytics.

What I care about: scale without manual toil. Most of my work ends in an automated pipeline, a self-service tool for non-technical teams, or a dashboard that replaces a recurring meeting. At Wayfair I pioneered Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), tracking visibility in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity and adapting content and schema so pages get cited by AI search. I build this kind of SEO tooling with Claude Code, custom Python crawlers, and the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs.

Outside of work I build at hackathons. I was runner-up at the Cyprus Hackathon with Workwise, an AI agent app. I read widely on culture, travel, and how systems get built.
